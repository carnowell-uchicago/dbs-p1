A Charming Straw Escape!

Pig One’s Straw Cottage

Exterior View — Straw Cottage
Meadow Road, Northern Plains
Est. One Day, Early September

Built in just one day by the industrious Pig One, this rustic straw retreat offers an authentic pastoral experience unlike anything found in the modern city. Light, airy, and proudly eco-conscious! Every wall breathes with character. Every room hums with the gentle rustle of nature.

Perfect for the traveler who appreciates speed, efficiency, and an open relationship with the outdoors.

Notice to All Visitors

🐾 Wolf Sighting Report — Oct. 3rd

On the evening of October 3rd, a large wolf was observed applying considerable respiratory force to the exterior of the Brick Residence. The structure was unaffected. The wolf was not. He was last seen retreating north at speed.

✓  Area Security Status

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

How to Get There

Follow Meadow Road heading north past the two vacant lots (formerly Stops 1 and 2). Continue until you see a very solid-looking brick house. You will know it immediately. It will look like it has seen things.

What to Bring

Warm clothes. A good attitude. Bricks, if you’re thinking of building anything.
