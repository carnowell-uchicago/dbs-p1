Classified Addendum — Eyes Only
Final Classified Note — Mission Director's Eyes Only
This mission confirms what Mission Specialist Bristle has been noting in internal memos since 2017: the only effective defense against a sustained, high-pressure asteroid impact event is a properly constructed, titanium-reinforced brick composite installation. Her four separate engineering reports on this subject have been located, read, and attached to this document as Exhibits A through D.

The SSA wishes to formally acknowledge that Mission Specialist Bristle was correct, and that the decision to proceed with HALMUM-1 and COMPMOD-2 as primary habitats represents a lapse in engineering judgment for which the Mission Director accepts full responsibility. The phrase "it'll probably be fine" has been removed from all SSA planning documents and replaced with "subject to Bristle structural review."

Mission Specialist Snout has submitted a formal request that his HALMUM-1 mission log entry reading "She'll hold. Probably." be redacted. This request has been declined. It has been framed and hung in the SSA Mission Planning conference room.

All three crew have been awarded the SSA Mission Achievement Medal. Mission Specialist Bristle has additionally been awarded the Medal for Applied Common Sense (first issuance; the medal was created specifically for this occasion).
