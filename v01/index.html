A Manifesto in Five Parts

Three
Little
Pigs

On shelter, threat, and what endures
Three pigs. One wolf. The question is not who will survive — it is what they built to do so.
Preface

The
World
Awaits

A mother sent her three pig children out into the world to make their own way. Each one would build a home. Each home would be tested.

The wolf was already waiting.
Doctrine I

Straw

The first pig built quickly. A house of straw — up in a day, cheap and done. He was proud. He rested. Then came the knock.
"LITTLE PIG, LITTLE PIG —
LET ME COME IN."
Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin.
"THEN I'LL HUFF,
AND I'LL PUFF."
It fell.
Doctrine II

Sticks

The second pig built with sticks. Harder to gather, sturdier than straw. Good enough, he thought. He laughed at his brother and bolted the door. Then came the knock.
"LITTLE PIG, LITTLE PIG —
LET ME COME IN."
Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin.
"THEN I'LL HUFF,
AND I'LL PUFF."
It fell.
Doctrine III

Bricks

The third pig built with bricks. It took days. Her brothers mocked her. She finished. Both brothers came running. Then came the knock.
"LITTLE PIG, LITTLE PIG —
LET ME COME IN."
Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin.
"THEN I'LL HUFF,
AND I'LL PUFF."
It stood.
Conclusion

Build
Well.

The wolf huffed until he had nothing left. He walked away. Inside the brick house, three pigs were warm, safe, and together.

What you build is what saves you.
Speed is not strength.
The threat is real.
