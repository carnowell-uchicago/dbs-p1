Three pig siblings have departed the family burrow to stake their own claims. Each intends to build a homestead suited to his means and ambitions. The territory watches.

Swine Valley Gazette · Est. 1849 Three pig siblings — Percival Snout, Barnabas Hoof, and Cornelius Bristle — have departed the family burrow to stake independent claims in the valley. Each intends to construct a homestead suited to his means and, in Snout's case, his profound optimism regarding the structural integrity of dried grass. The territory watches with appropriate skepticism.
