Section 04 — Retrospective

LESSONS LEARNED

What We Learned
  • · Speed is not a product strategy
  • · Minimum viable product is not minimum viable shelter
  • · "Good enough" has a wolf-resistance floor
  • · Your competitor's lung capacity is a real risk factor to model
  • · Pending certifications are not the same as certifications
Pivot
New Direction Both StrawHaus™ and StickStack® teams are merging operations. New direction: brick. BrickCore™ has agreed to absorb both founding teams and their remaining assets (a pile of sticks and some straw).
