Prologue
❧
Once upon a time, a mother pig sent her three sons out into the wide world to seek their fortunes and build homes of their very own.
The eldest was hasty. The middle, careless. The youngest — patient beyond her years.
And somewhere in the deep, dark wood, a wolf was already sharpening his breath.
·   ·   ·
Chapter the First
The House
of Straw
The eldest pig built with golden straw — quick to gather, easy to raise. By sundown his house was done. He laughed, and played, and rested, proud of his speed.
That evening, came the knock.
"Little pig, little pig — let me come in!"
"Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin!"
"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house down!"
And down it came.
Chapter the Second
The House
of Sticks
The second pig gathered sticks from the forest edge and lashed them together with rope and care. Sturdier than straw, he thought. Wiser than his brother's mistake.
But the wolf came again, patient and hungry.
"Little pig, little pig — let me come in!"
"Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin!"
"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house down!"
And down it came.
Chapter the Third
The House
of Bricks
The youngest pig laid her bricks one by one, mortar-thick and true. Her brothers mocked her slow hands. She finished anyway — and both brothers came running when their houses fell.
Then the wolf came for the very last time.
"Little pig, little pig — let me come in!"
"Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin!"
"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house down!"
The house stood firm.
Epilogue
❧
The wolf huffed until he had nothing left. He walked away, cold and alone, back into the wood from which he'd come.
Inside the warm brick house, three little pigs sat together by a crackling fire, eating their supper and listening to the wind outside — which was only the wind.
